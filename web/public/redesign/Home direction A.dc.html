How it works

  1. 01

    Get a Truchsess

    A small computer in your office. Your workers run on it, each in its own sandbox.

  2. 02

    Connect it to the store

    One code from Old School links your appliance to this catalog. Once.

  3. 03

    Subscribe on the appliance

    Pay monthly through Polar, which handles VAT and invoices. Your appliance never sees card data.

  4. 04

    Approve and install

    You see every permission in plain words first. Cancel any time: the worker is removed with a signed receipt.

What "governed" means

Four rules your appliance enforces for every worker from this store.

Signed, or not installed

Every worker is signed with Old School's publisher key. Your appliance checks the signature and the package digest before it installs anything.

Only what you allow

A worker declares what it needs, for example "read one repository". Your policy decides. Nothing else is possible.

Your files stay with you

Files and results stay on your appliance. Only the model call leaves it, through the provider you choose.

Every run on the record

Status, cost and approvals of each run are logged on your appliance, in your budget.
