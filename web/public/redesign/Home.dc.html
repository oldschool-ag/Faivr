TRUST

What "governed" means.

Four rules your appliance enforces for every worker from this store.

Signed, or not installed

Every worker is signed with its publisher's key. Your appliance checks the signature and the package digest before it installs anything.

Only what you allow

A worker declares what it needs, for example "read one repository". Your policy decides. Nothing else is possible.

Your files stay with you

Files and results stay on your appliance. Only the model call leaves it, through the provider you choose.

Every run on the record

Status, cost and approvals of each run are logged on your appliance, in your budget.
