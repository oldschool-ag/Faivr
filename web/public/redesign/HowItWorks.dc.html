HOW IT WORKS

Your appliance. Your rules. Our workers.

FAIVR workers run on Truchsess, a small computer in your office. This page shows the four steps from order to first result, and what you need.

You need

  • A Truchsess appliance
  • A network cable and internet access
  • A model account, or our managed one
  1. 1

    Get a Truchsess

    We install a small computer in your office and set up your company: you as owner, your team as members, your products. Every worker runs on it in its own sandbox.

    Who does what

    Old School sets it up with you. Updates arrive signed and are installed from your admin page.

  2. 2

    Connect it to the store

    You get a one-time code from Old School. You paste it once on your appliance, under Administration, Packages, Store. From then on your appliance sees this catalog.

    What the store learns

    An ID for your appliance and its public key. Nothing from your files or runs.

  3. 3

    Subscribe on the appliance

    Pick a function and click Subscribe. Your browser opens the payment page of Polar, our merchant of record. Pay by card. Back on your appliance, the function shows as active within seconds.

    Billing

    Monthly, Swiss VAT included, invoice by Polar. Your appliance never sees card data.

  4. 4

    Approve and install

    Your appliance shows every permission in plain words and compares it with your policy. Click Install. The signed package is downloaded, checked and installed in seconds. Then give the worker its first task in the chat.

    Cancel any time

    Uninstall the worker or cancel on your appliance. It runs until the end of the paid month, then it is removed with a signed receipt.

What you pay for

Two costs, both visible on your appliance.

FUNCTION

A fixed monthly price

One price per function, for example Design review. It covers the worker, its updates and the store.

MODEL USE

The AI model, as used

Each task calls an AI model. Your appliance shows the cost of every run and stops at the monthly budget you set.
