Before you subscribe

Why can't I buy here?

A worker needs your appliance to run. You subscribe on your Truchsess, so the worker is installed in the same step, with the permissions you approve.

How do I cancel?

Uninstall the worker or cancel on your appliance. The subscription ends with the paid month, and you get a signed removal receipt.

Who sends the invoice?

Polar, our merchant of record. Every invoice shows the Swiss VAT. Card data never reaches FAIVR or your appliance.
