WHAT IVO DOES

You give him a page. He gives you a brief.

  1. 1

    Renders the page

    Opens it in a browser inside his sandbox and takes screenshots. If your allowlist blocks parts of the page, he says so instead of guessing.

  2. 2

    Reviews what he sees

    Clarity, usability, accessibility, trust, responsive quality, and whether the page looks like a template.

  3. 3

    Writes the brief

    Findings by severity, concrete revisions and a verdict, with the screenshots, saved as files on your appliance.

EXAMPLE TASK

Render https://example.com and describe the page, attach a screenshot.

Result: a brief and screenshots, usually within minutes.

PERMISSIONS
