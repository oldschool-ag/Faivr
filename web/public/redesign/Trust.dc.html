TRUST

Trust you can check, not trust we ask for.

Every rule on this page is enforced by your own appliance. You can see it work on your admin page.

Four rules for every worker

Signed, or not installed

Every package is signed with its publisher's key. Your appliance checks the signature and the package digest before it installs anything, and refuses on any mismatch.

Only what you allow

A worker declares what it needs. Your policy decides. Websites outside your allowlist stay closed, and a worker cannot change its own permissions.

Nothing on its own

Workers act only on a task someone in your company started. No background jobs, no self-updates, no hidden model calls.

Every run on the record

Status, cost and approvals of each run are logged on your appliance. Your budget stops runs when it is used up.

PUBLISHERS

You decide whom your appliance trusts.

Each package names its publisher and carries that publisher's signature. Your appliance keeps a list of trusted publishers, and only you can add one.

Old School reviews every package before it enters this catalog.
